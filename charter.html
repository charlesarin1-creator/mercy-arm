MERCY ARM CHARTER

Governance • Stewardship • James 1:27

Dashboard Charter

Preamble

“Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress and to keep oneself from being polluted by the world.” — James 1:27

Mercy ARM exists as a stewardship structure for governed mercy, accountability, and covenant-based assistance to widows and orphans.

Foundational Doctrine

Mercy must remain:

  • Structured
  • Traceable
  • Sustainable
  • Governed
  • Free from spectacle

The system exists to resist emotional drift, silent disorder, and unstructured compassion.

Governance Structure

Founder-Steward: Oversees all mercy allocation and preservation rules.

Witness: Reviews distributions, exceptions, and quarterly integrity.

Mercy Ledger: Permanent memory layer recording every distribution.

Vault Preservation Principle

A minimum of 30% of the Mercy Vault balance remains untouched at all times.

Sustainability is prioritized over emotional depletion.

Exception Rule

Any mercy distribution outside written eligibility rules must be marked as EXCEPTION within the ledger.

Exceptions remain visible, reviewable, and accountable.

Operational Philosophy

Mercy ARM is not designed for public spectacle.

It operates as a governed mercy system:

  • No public fundraising campaigns
  • No emotional manipulation
  • No uncontrolled expansion
  • No automation authority over mercy decisions
