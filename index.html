What is Mercy ARM?

Mercy ARM is a structured mercy support system designed to provide assistance to widows and orphans in real distress.

It is built on accountability, simplicity, and governed compassion.

Who Can Receive Help?

  • Widows without financial support
  • Orphans under 18 without parental care

No other categories are accepted at this stage.

How It Works

  • Submit a request
  • Your case enters review queue
  • Verification is done internally
  • Approved cases are contacted directly
